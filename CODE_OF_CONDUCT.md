# Code of conduct

Be civil and stay on topic. Ask, answer and disagree about AyMON, not about
people.

Not welcome here: insults, harassment, spam, advertising, and anything posted
to get around a plant's security rather than to fix it.

This space is looked after by one person, who will hide, lock or delete what
does not fit and block accounts that keep doing it — without a long
discussion. To report something, use **Report content** on the post, or write
to support@aymon.ca.
