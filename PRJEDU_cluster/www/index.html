Cluster APP/S00 on S01 and S02

master: --term --
Try it
  • Stop the master: CTRL-C in its window (or close it). The other column turns MASTER within a few seconds and the count goes on from where it was.
  • Start it again: it comes back as SPARE (pro_clt_preempt="0": a live master is not pushed aside), pulls the job once and follows.
  • Hand over on the master, or Take over on the spare: a planned switch, no engine stopped.
  • Change the rate on the SPARE's column: the spare does not apply it - it forwards it to the master, and the new value comes back by replication.
