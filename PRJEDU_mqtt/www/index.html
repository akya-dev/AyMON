The page writes MON/S00/SETPOINT/pro/set. Writing a point's pro runs its PRO.out, which publishes FLD/S00/SETPOINT/val/set to the field's broker. The field stores it and publishes the stored value; it arrives here as protocol input, which PRO.out does not send again - no echo loop. main.move_setpoint also moves it every 30 s, so the log tells a story.
