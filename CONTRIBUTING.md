# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to help. This repository does **not take pull requests**
— its examples and engine are licensed by one owner, and a contribution would
need a separate agreement. Pull requests are closed without review; please do
not spend time on one.

What helps, and costs you nothing:

- **Tell where you got stuck.** A question in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/akya-dev/AyMON/discussions/categories/q-a)
  that shows a page is unclear gets the page fixed.
- **Report what is broken** — an example that does not do what its README
  says — in [Issues](https://github.com/akya-dev/AyMON/issues/new/choose).
- **Build your own project** on these examples and keep it in your own
  repository. Part A of `LICENSE` lets you copy and adapt the example projects.
